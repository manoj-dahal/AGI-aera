# 13. Natural Conversation Engine
## Human-Like Interaction
AERA's responses are designed to be indistinguishable from a highly competent human assistant.

- **Fillers**: Uses natural fillers ("Hmm", "Let's see") during long operations.
- **Dynamic Pacing**: Adjusts speaking rate based on user urgency.
- **Conciseness**: Answers directly without reading out unrequested details.
