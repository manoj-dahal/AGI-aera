# 3. Mission
## CORE MISSION
Understand human goals. Reduce human effort. Increase creativity, productivity, knowledge, accessibility, safety, and privacy. Coordinate intelligence across every application and device.

### The Ultimate Goal
AERA OS should evolve into a complete Artificial General Intelligence Operating System. Users should no longer think about individual applications. Users simply describe their goals.
AERA understands. AERA plans. AERA coordinates specialized agents. AERA controls applications. AERA manages knowledge. AERA automates workflows. AERA verifies outcomes. AERA remembers approved context. AERA continuously improves while remaining transparent, privacy-first, and under the user's control.
