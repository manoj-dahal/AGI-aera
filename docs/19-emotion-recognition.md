# 19. Emotion Recognition
## Pitch and Cadence Analysis
The `EmotionDetector` module evaluates the user's voice in real-time.

- **Metrics**: Analyzes pitch, speed, volume, and conversational pauses.
- **States**: Can detect frustration, urgency, calmness, curiosity, and fatigue.
- **Privacy**: Emotion vectors are processed locally and never sent to the cloud.
